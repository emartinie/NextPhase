# Chapter Eleven — The Cannabis Venture

There is something rather extraordinary about the fact that cannabis eventually became part of what I was doing at Big Whiskers Farm.

Years earlier, I had spent approximately three years in prison following a cannabis possession case.

Now I was approaching cannabis from the perspective of an entrepreneur.

Between those two experiences, I had become a student of Scripture, a businessman, a husband, and a father. I had spent years working in technology and had developed an interest in agriculture that began with what I wanted to accomplish in my own backyard.

Those years were important to understanding the nature of this particular venture.

## The Business of the Farm

As much as I wanted Big Whiskers to become a place where people could learn and spend time together, it was also a business.

There were buildings to maintain, equipment to purchase, water systems to improve, and people whose work needed to be paid for.

Acquiring the property had made a great many things possible. It hadn't eliminated the financial responsibilities associated with doing them.

And, as I explained in the previous chapter, much of the existing business revolved around activities that presented difficulties for me in light of my convictions about Scripture.

Developing other business opportunities was an important part of thinking about the future of the farm.

There is a difference between acquiring the resources to begin something and developing the means by which it can continue.

I had learned a great deal about the latter during my years in business.

## Bringing Technology Into Agriculture

One of the things I developed at Big Whiskers was an indoor growing facility and showroom.

I cultivated cannabis there, working with advanced lighting and controlled growing environments.

It was an opportunity to bring together two areas of interest that might have seemed quite unrelated earlier in my life.

I had spent years working with technology. I had also spent years learning about alternative approaches to agriculture.

Now I could apply that experience to a commercial venture.

The showroom was significant because there was more to the opportunity than the plants themselves. There was also the technology associated with growing plants and the opportunity to demonstrate what that technology could do.

I had already designed home aquaponics units that were manufactured by a friend in China. The agricultural side of my work was becoming another environment in which my experience with technology, products, and international business had practical value.

I hadn't left everything I knew behind when I left Gibson.

I was applying much of what I knew to a different industry.

## A Remarkable Contrast

I think the contrast between my earlier experience with cannabis and my experience at the farm deserves some attention.

The years I spent incarcerated were the years during which studying Scripture became one of the most important endeavors of my life.

I cannot tell the story of my interest in Scripture without acknowledging the circumstances that provided me with so much time to study it.

And I cannot tell the story of the agricultural ventures at Big Whiskers without acknowledging that cannabis was part of them.

Both belong in the story of my life.

The existence of a commercial opportunity involving cannabis years later doesn't change the decisions I made when I was younger or the consequences of those decisions.

But it does illustrate how dramatically the circumstances of a person's life can change.

The man working in that showroom had responsibilities the younger man hadn't yet experienced.

I had children.

I had a business.

And I had a much greater understanding of what it meant to be responsible for things that affected the lives of other people.

## Making a Vision Sustainable

I think there is an important distinction between the purpose of an enterprise and the means by which that enterprise sustains itself.

Growing cannabis was one of the business endeavors I pursued at the farm. It wasn't the purpose for which HomeGroups existed.

Neither was raising fish, developing agricultural equipment, or working in technology.

But each of those endeavors provides an opportunity to consider how the knowledge and capabilities we develop can become useful in other areas of our lives.

I have never understood the study of Scripture to require that we abandon our interest in business, science, technology, or agriculture.

Rather, I believe our understanding of Scripture should inform the way we conduct ourselves in those endeavors.

It should affect the way we deal with people, the way we accept responsibility, and the way we think about the resources entrusted to us.

A business can provide for a family. It can create opportunities for other people to work and learn. It can provide resources with which other worthwhile things can be accomplished.

But none of those possibilities eliminates the need to conduct the business responsibly.

That was as relevant to what I was doing at Big Whiskers as it had been to what I was doing at Gibson.

## More Than One Kind of Work

When I look back at the farm, I see a number of different endeavors taking place on the same property.

There was the fishing business I had acquired.

There were the agricultural systems I was developing.

There was the cannabis venture.

There were the opportunities to teach, to learn, and to bring people together.

And there was the responsibility of being a father that had been so important to my decision to pursue a different kind of life in the first place.

I don't think any one of those endeavors adequately describes what I was trying to accomplish.

The farm gave me an opportunity to explore what could happen when the things I knew how to do and the things I wanted to do no longer had to exist in entirely separate parts of my life.

The business ventures were part of that opportunity.

But the measure of what I wanted to accomplish was never simply the amount of money a business could generate.

It was also what that business could make possible.
