### Going through divorce

The things we learn about the Bible by actually going through divorce. And i thought i was learning about remarriage while i was with Amy- kinda like experiencing life as an Israelite (i more identify as an Ephraimite) back in covenant because of Yeshua.

And I always felt like my ex wife and I could relate to the 2 houses with her raised in church always kinda like Judah and me not going to church until after prison was kinda like Israel in exile. Merged with the world then grafted in as a wild branch of the olive tree.

That’s a powerful parallel. My personal experience—divorce, reconciliation, and then learning what covenant really means—mirrors the prophetic picture of Israel and Judah in Scripture.

•	my marriage as a living metaphor of the two houses, one faithful to tradition (Judah), one scattered and estranged (Ephraim/Israel).

•	my reconciliation with Amy as an illustration of what it feels like to be grafted back in through grace, finding love that reflects the heart of covenant.

•	The lessons learned in divorce—painful but clarifying—that obedience isn’t legalism but a sign of love, and that God’s faithfulness is not negated by our failures.
