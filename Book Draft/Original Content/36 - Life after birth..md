### Life after birth.

I wish my kids understood how much I love them every second of EVERY day.

I also wish to share with them an analogy of life before and after their birth to help them think more deeply about life before and after death…

Imagine they were both twins in my wife’s womb even though they weren’t, and imagine they were capable of having full conversations shortly after I placed our kids into my wife’s womb…

Imagine thinking that life inside the womb must be the only life that will ever exist because it’s the only life you’ve ever known.
