### Chapter 5 – Purpose and Calling

For much of my life, I thought purpose was something you eventually discovered.

I imagined it waiting somewhere in the future, like a destination I would recognize once I finally arrived.

Looking back, I see it differently.

Purpose was there from the beginning.

I simply hadn't learned to recognize it.

### The Thread

When I look across the different seasons of my life, I see a common thread connecting experiences that once seemed completely unrelated.

Technology.

Leadership.

Business.

Teaching.

Writing.

Building communities.

Helping people.

At the time, each chapter felt separate. Only in hindsight did I realize they were all expressions of the same calling to help people.

The tools changed.

The mission did not.

### More Than a Career

Our culture often defines people by what they do.

"What do you do for a living?"

It's usually one of the first questions we ask.

But a career and a calling are not the same thing.

Careers change.

Companies come and go.

Titles disappear.

A calling survives every transition.

It remains when jobs end, businesses fail, dreams change, or life takes an unexpected turn.

I have experienced each of those moments.

The calling remained.

### Through Every Season

There were seasons of success.

There were seasons of loss.

There were moments when I felt certain I was exactly where I was supposed to be.

There were other moments when everything I had built seemed to collapse around me.

Yet through every season there was a quiet reminder that refused to disappear:

You were made for more.

Not more success.

Not more possessions.

More purpose.

The difficult seasons didn't erase my calling.

They refined it.

### Living with Purpose

Today I no longer believe purpose is something we stumble across by accident or create ourselves.

I believe it is something we choose to live every day.

Sometimes it looks dramatic.

More often it looks ordinary.

Helping one person.

Solving one problem.

Building one meaningful thing.

Speaking one word of encouragement.

Faithfulness in small things eventually becomes the story of a lifetime.

### Reflection

If God places a calling within each of us, then purpose is not about becoming someone else.

It is about becoming the person He created us to be.

The journey is rarely straight.

It often includes failure, disappointment, unexpected detours, and seasons of waiting.

But none of those things are wasted.

Each one becomes another thread in the story God is writing.
