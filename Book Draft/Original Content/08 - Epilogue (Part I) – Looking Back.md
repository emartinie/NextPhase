### Epilogue (Part I) – Looking Back

When I look back across the years, I don't see a straight line.

I see questions.

I see victories.

I see failures.

I see seasons that made no sense while I was living them but now fit together in ways I never could have imagined.

If I could speak to the young man carrying that notebook full of questions, I don't think I would give him many answers.

Instead, I would tell him to keep asking.

### The Journey Was the Teacher

There were times I believed I had finally figured everything out.

Then life would humble me.

A new experience.

A painful loss.

A relationship.

A failure.

A discovery hidden in Scripture.

Each one reminded me that truth is too large to be possessed.

It must be pursued.

The older I become, the less interested I am in winning arguments.

I'm far more interested in understanding people, listening carefully, and allowing evidence to reshape my thinking whenever necessary.

That isn't weakness;

It's growth.

### What Remains

Careers change.

Businesses rise and fall.

Dreams evolve.

People come and go.

But some things endure.

Faith.

Hope.

Love.

Purpose.

The conviction that every life has value.

The belief that redemption is possible.

The certainty that God is still writing stories long after we believe the chapter has ended.

Those are the things I carry with me today.

### The Search Continues

People sometimes ask whether I have finally found the truth.

My answer surprises them.

Yes.

And no.

I know the One who called Himself the Truth.

But knowing Him hasn't ended my search.

It has transformed it.

Now I don't study to prove myself right or others wrong.

I study because wonder has replaced certainty.

Curiosity has replaced fear.

The search continues—not only because truth is often hidden, but because it is inexhaustible.

Every page of Scripture.

Every conversation.

Every hardship.

Every blessing.

Each reveals another glimpse of the Creator.

### A Final Thought

If this book has one message, I hope it is this:

Don't be afraid of difficult questions.

Don't be afraid to admit when you're wrong.

Don't be afraid to change my mind when the evidence leads somewhere unexpected.

Truth has nothing to fear from honest investigation.

And neither should we.

The journey isn't over.

In many ways...

It's only just beginning.

There are seasons in life when you become convinced that some chapters have ended forever.

After my divorce, I believed love was one of those chapters.

I had poured years into building a marriage, a family, a home, and a future. When those dreams collapsed, I wasn't looking for another relationship. I was simply trying to survive.

Then I met Amy at the lawyer’s office one her way back to the town where we met.

### An Unexpected Beginning

What began as a friendship quickly became something much deeper.

Our conversations stretched for hours.

We traveled together.

We explored new places.

We spent quiet mornings on the farm and evenings talking about life, faith, books, and the future.

For the first time in a long time, I felt understood.

Not because we agreed about everything, but because we genuinely enjoyed discovering who the other person was.

### Rewriting the Story

One of the dreams we shared was writing a novel together.

We called it History Rewritten.

It wasn't intended to be an autobiography.

It was fiction inspired by real emotions.

The central question was simple:

What if two people who should have found each other much earlier were given another chance?

That question became the heartbeat of the story.

Rather than changing the past, the novel explored how memory, identity, love, regret, and hope shape the people we become.

It asked whether our lives are defined more by what happened—or by what we choose after it happens.

### Growing Together

Amy encouraged me in ways I hadn't experienced before.

She challenged me to keep learning about taking care of myself.

To think more carefully.

To become more emotionally aware.

Many of our conversations centered on communication, relationships, personal growth, and understanding ourselves as honestly as possible.

Those discussions changed me.

Not because someone was trying to fix me.

Because someone believed I was still becoming.

### Lessons from Love

Looking back, I realize the relationship gave me far more than happy memories.

It reminded me that healing is possible.

That hope can return after heartbreak.

That love is not simply something we find.

It is something we continually choose to cultivate.

Whether that chapter ultimately ended the way I once imagined is almost secondary now.

Its greatest gift was reminding me that my story wasn't over.

### Reflection

Some people enter our lives for a season.

Others change the direction of our lives forever.

Regardless of how long they stay, both can become part of the story God is writing.

Love doesn't erase pain.

But it often teaches us that pain doesn't have to write the final chapter.
