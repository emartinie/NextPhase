### On anger

Most people don’t realize it unless they’ve healed from an angry partner or marriage but anger is rarely just anger.

It’s a way for the ‘angry’ person to keep the truth buried beneath the surface…Because the truth apparently hurts them.

It’s like a kick in the nutz with a frozen boot, when you realize you aren't who you wanted to be or went to school to be or whatever didn’t work out.

It hurts maybe to feel like time has slipped through your fingers.

It hurts maybe to wake up and know—deep down—that you’ve been living small when you could have been living large….You’re just one of the herd; Following aimlessly and that kind of pain, maybe

It’s unbearable. So we get angry.

We lash out.

We blame others.

We convince ourselves that the world is unfair, that life is the problem or our spouse is the problem. Maybe we even cheat on the person that we get angry with.

But wanna know the truth,

The real truth, No bull?

Our anger isn’t about them. Their anger isn’t about us.

Our frustration isn’t about the world. It’s about ourselves. for example, women use social media for immediate gratification the same as men use porn for the same thing even though one is looked on worse than the other even though it’s the same thing.

If we are being truthful It’s about us.

It’s about the life you know you were meant to live but haven’t.

It’s about the dreams you buried because fear told you to wait.It’s about the grief of looking at yourself and knowing you were made for more.

And I get it. I’ve been there. And I’m there again now.  I’ve felt it in my soul—the rage, the resentment, the feeling of being trapped inside a life that wasn’t mine; it's like you're looking at life through a fishbowl.

But when I finally sat with it, when I finally looked inside myself I realised It wasn’t anger. It was sadness. It was grief. And I haven’t been angry really in probably 25 years or more. But I married an angry woman so I learned even more about anger watching from afar.

But here’s what I need you to hear: It’s not too late. You can still change everything.

You can still take back your life.

Because if you dont deal with what hurt you, you will bleed on the people that didn't cut you.

Are you really angry? Or are you grieving?

Because if it’s grief, then anger won’t fix it. The only way out is through. We either get bitter…Or we get better. So sit with yourself. Ask the hard questions. It’s ok to be angry but are we angry, or are we confused?
