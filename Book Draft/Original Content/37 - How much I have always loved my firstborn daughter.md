### How much I have always loved my firstborn daughter

The first time I saw you I knew I’d love you forever

I knew that I had someone special and we would always be together

Nothing can separate or come between us, nothing can infiltrate our bond

You are my best girl ever to infinity and beyond

You are my EVERYTHING and your brother is too. But NOTHING can change that my first-born is you

Now you’re both all grown after all these years and no words can describe how I fight back the tears

I’m proud and I’m sad, I’m so grateful for all time we have had

My life is bittersweet ever since we had the chance to meet

And even though right now I’m not totally the man I used to be, I’m still and always am your daddy, for eternity

Nothing can change that; nothing at all… as long as I’m alive I’m someone you can call

For any thing and for any reason. No matter the time and no matter the season; my reason for living is you and your brother too

One day you’ll realize and have a family of your own, one day you’ll learn how important it is to build and have a home

But a house is just a house if you don’t have a family inside. Without a spouse and kids, it’s just a place to reside.

So make your house a HOME and learn from my mistakes. Learn to live your life without having any regrets; make it great for hapiness’ sake.

You mean the world to me and I hope you have no doubt. If you do it’s okay, one day you’ll figure that out.

But I think you know because you know so much; you know how much you mean to me, you bring me happiness and such

And so much more if you only knew, but I know one day you’ll figure will

One day you’ll have your own ‘best ever’ and it will be such a great big deal!

It will change your life forever and things will never be the same
