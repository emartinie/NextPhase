### Narcissistic abuse

I had been reeling from the divorce for months, struggling to make sense of the tumultuous relationship me and the kids had endured for years. My friend Jason, who had been through a similar experience, sat me down one day and said, "Eddie, I want to tell you something that might change everything for you."

Jason explained the concept of narcissistic abuse, describing how some individuals use manipulation, gaslighting, and emotional exploitation to control their partners. As Jason shared his own experiences and insights, a light bulb went off in my head.

I began to see his ex-wife's behavior in a new light – the constant criticism, the emotional outbursts, the way she made him feel small and insignificant. It wasn't because he was flawed or inadequate, as he had been led to believe. It was because she had been using narcissistic tactics to maintain power over him.

I felt a mix of emotions – anger, sadness, and relief – as the puzzle pieces of his marriage finally fell into place. He realized that he hadn't been the problem; I had been a victim of narcissistic abuse.

With this newfound understanding, I began to heal and rebuild his sense of self-worth. He realized that he deserved to be treated with respect, kindness, and empathy – and that he had the strength to set boundaries and protect himself from toxic relationships in the future.

Thanks to Jason's guidance and support, I emerged from the darkness of his past with a renewed sense of purpose and a deeper understanding of himself and others. He was grateful for the awakening, painful as it was, and vowed to use his experience to help others who might be trapped in similar situations.
