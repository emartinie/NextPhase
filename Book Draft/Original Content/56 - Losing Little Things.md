#### Losing Little Things

It's funny what we mourn.

Nobody tells you that one day you'll spend half your life putting your glasses on... and then taking them right back off.

I'll be sitting at my computer trying to read something on the monitor, so the glasses come off. Then my phone buzzes, so the glasses go back on. Then I need to look across the room, so they come off again. Five minutes later, I'm looking for the very glasses that are sitting on top of my head.

It's such a little thing.

Compared to everything else life can throw at you, it's almost laughable.

But it also reminds me that growing older isn't usually one dramatic event. It's a thousand tiny adjustments. One day you realize your knees make noises they never used to make. Another day you discover you can't read the ingredients on a soup can without better lighting. Eventually you stop fighting it and just start leaving reading glasses everywhere—in the truck, beside the bed, in the kitchen, at work—because you know you're going to need them.

It's not tragic.

It's just life.

And if you're reading this without glasses, don't worry.

Your turn is probably coming.
