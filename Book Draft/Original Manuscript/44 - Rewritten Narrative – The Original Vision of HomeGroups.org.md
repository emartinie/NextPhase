### Rewritten Narrative – The Original Vision of HomeGroups.org

It began as more than an idea—it was a burden on my heart that wouldn’t let me sleep.

Long before anyone was talking about digital communities or decentralized church networks, I saw what was coming. The traditional church was fracturing, losing its influence, losing its mission. People were drifting—some leaving faith altogether, others just quietly starving while sitting in pews. I felt it in my bones: the church needed to come back to the homes, to the people, to the real heartbeat of discipleship.

That was the seed of HomeGroups.org. I imagined a digital platform that wasn’t just another website, but a living network of believers, a place where teaching, prayer, and fellowship could thrive without denominational walls or political agendas. It was Ezekiel 37 playing out in real time—dry bones scattered across a spiritual valley, waiting for breath.

At the time, I didn’t have the resources, just a vision and a conviction. I prayed, I wrote, I sketched out how it would work. There would be tools for leaders to connect with their communities, ways for seekers to ask hard questions without fear, and a global map of home gatherings so anyone, anywhere, could find believers nearby. I dreamed of an ecosystem where teaching wasn’t about fear or control, but about raising up mature disciples—rooted in love, morality, and sound theology.

When social media started exploding, I realized it could be leveraged to spread this vision. Back in 2007 and 2008, while most churches still saw Facebook as a distraction, I saw it as a mission field. I created dozens of secret groups, began posting news and prophetic warnings that people said were “crazy” at the time. But I knew what I was seeing. Americans—and believers—needed to wake up.

HomeGroups was my response to both a spiritual crisis and a cultural one. It was never about building my brand or making money (God knows I’ve never taken a dime for this work). It was about unity. About taking back what the enemy had scattered. About preparing the Body for the awakening that I knew was coming.

Looking back now, the vision was ahead of its time. People thought it was radical, unnecessary, maybe even foolish. But today, as I revisit those early words, I see how much of it was prophetic. It was a call to arms—not with weapons, but with truth, with love, with the kind of community that can’t be broken.

Chapter: The Awakening – Q, Patriots, and the Digital Frontline

The first thing I want anyone who reads this to know is simple: I have never made a dime off of my work.

In all my years of website development, mobile app creation, and countless hours of research and posting, I have never monetized what I’ve done. I have never asked for donations, never sold Q hats or shirts, never sought to profit from what I considered a mission. Unlike so many “PAYtriots” who turned this into a business, my only investment has been my own time and resources. That is my starting point and my distinction.

The Early Vision

Before Q ever appeared, before “The Great Awakening” became a phrase, I was already there.

Years earlier, God had put a vision on my heart: to wake people up gently, to call Americans—especially Christian Americans—out of their slumber. I posted, I built secret Facebook groups (fifty of them), and I began pushing news and information that most people thought was crazy. I remember when the term “conspiracy theorist” was the easy label to shut someone down. But I also remember how many times, over the years, what we said would happen actually did.

The government’s overreach was everywhere:

•	Telling us what to eat, drink, and smoke.

•	Forcing vaccinations and radiation scans.

•	Using our tax money for things we opposed.

•	Criminalizing self-sufficiency like home gardens.

•	Chipping away at every freedom God gave us.

Even then, I knew this wasn’t about left vs. right. It was about freedom vs. control, truth vs. lies.

Conversations with Patriots

My posts stirred up a lot. Some friends vanished from my life. Others came forward with encouragement:

Rachel wrote:

“It’s sad most people just want to sit back & not face the facts. They don’t want to stop & stand up. Keep up with telling the truth. People will listen soon…”

Nathan told me:

“Good people don’t want to believe others could be so corrupt. They’ll call it conspiracy because they can’t handle the truth. But one day, you’ll be vindicated in everything you’ve said…”

Hugh reminded me:

“You’re too well-educated and informed for most of the asleep sheep. Keep doing what you do. Your real friends will stay.”

I responded to them:

I find it amazing that anyone would say I don’t provide facts. That’s all I post. I’ve been documenting the lies for years: the fake birth certificate, the CIA’s role in Al-Qaeda, the warnings about Agenda 21, the proof of false flag events like Vietnam. I said years ago the dollar would collapse, that lobbyists own our Congress, that the bankers were the real enemy. And now? These things are finally surfacing.

Q Arrives

When Q first showed up, I was skeptical.

I was already juggling dozens of sources, sifting for truth in the noise. But Q was different. Slowly, what they dropped lined up with what I had been tracking for years. This wasn’t a random group of posters. This was military intelligence, communicating directly with the American people to bypass the controlled media. They were dropping crumbs, and we—anonymous patriots, “autists,” researchers—were following them.

Q asked questions, never gave easy answers. They forced us to dig, to think.

The message was clear: You, the PEOPLE, have the POWER. They want you divided. Stay strong. Stay together.

For the first time, I didn’t feel alone in this fight.

The same fire God had placed in me years earlier was now burning across the world.

The Biblical Thread

Through it all, I saw the pattern: this was Biblical.

The great awakening wasn’t just political; it was prophetic. Q drops often included Scripture. I realized that the movement—whether the team fully understood it or not—was part of what God had already declared. This was Ezekiel’s vision playing out in modern times: dead bones coming to life, unity where there was once division.

I prayed through it all.

During Shavuot, I prayed for Q Team, veterans, and leaders in all fifty states. I called them Qmers instead of Omers. I asked God to protect them, to guide them, to use them.

Enemies in the Shadows

The Cabal, the Deep State, whatever you call it—it’s real.

We saw proof in the connections between the CIA, the Vatican, human trafficking, the Podestas, and beyond. We saw the globalist agenda, the 16-year plan to destroy America. We saw how they worship darkness. Epstein’s island wasn’t a myth; it was just one doorway into the underbelly of the world’s evil.

I understood something crucial: This was not about politics. This was about good versus evil.

My Stand

I was attacked, censored, banned.

I watched friends walk away because they couldn’t face the truth. But I kept posting. I kept teaching.

And I kept saying what Q said:

“Read the BIBLE. GOD WINS.”

Today’s Reflection

Looking back now, I see it clearly:

Q was never my savior. Trump was never my savior. No movement, no man, no government will ever be my savior.

God is my savior.

But I also believe God used Q—just as He has used flawed people and broken vessels throughout history—to wake people up, to shake them out of their complacency, and to bring light into darkness.

This chapter is not about proving Q is real.

It’s about showing how I—and many others—walked through that season, learned what was hidden, and fought for truth. It’s about the awakening of both the world and the soul.

The Awakening – The Voice of Patriots

As Q’s movement began to ripple across the world, I was already deep in the fight. My voice was loud—sometimes too loud for friends who wanted to stay comfortable. Facebook became my battleground long before Q drops.

I didn’t care about losing followers; I cared about truth.

I cared about waking people up, even if they hated me for it.

The Patriot Party Conversations

I remember the month it all came to a head. I was posting non-stop, sharing articles, exposing lies, and connecting dots most people didn’t want to see. The conversations from that time still echo in my head:

“Mainstream media portrays fed-up Americans as crazy or racist to divide us.

This has nothing to do with left or right.

When that doesn’t work, they bring up the race card.”

I said it then, and I’ll say it again: WHO CARES what color people are?

The Tea Party, Patriots, black, white—it didn’t matter. What mattered was freedom.

The government had become a tyrant, dictating what we could eat, what we could teach our kids, what we could grow in our gardens. They taxed us, spied on us, and sold us out to lobbyists. They radiated us at airports, forced medical decisions, and silenced dissent. That wasn’t democracy; that was control.

The Truth Few Wanted to Hear

I wrote:

“And to say that lower taxes is impossible, just look at history.

We did fine before WWI.

Then 1914—POW!

The Federal Reserve Act of 1917 changed everything.

The bankers took over. They own our Congress. They own our money. They own our future.

THAT is the enemy of true Americans.”

I warned people: the New World Order is real.

I may be a globalist by trade, but I will never support a global system that destroys sovereignty and enslaves its people.

Voices That Encouraged Me

During those posts, messages started pouring in:

•	Rachel:

“It’s sad most people don’t want to face the facts. Keep telling the truth, Eddie. People will listen soon.”

•	Nathan:

“Don’t get discouraged. Conspiracy sites are often better researched than mainstream media. One day you’ll be vindicated.”

•	Hugh:

“You’re too educated and informed for the asleep sheep. Keep going. Your real friends will stay.”

Their encouragement reminded me that the battle was worth it.

I replied to them:

“I’ve been documenting this for years. The things I posted long ago—about the dollar collapse, about Agenda 21, about Obama’s fake birth certificate, about CIA-Al-Qaeda ties—they’re not theories. They’re facts. They’re now admitted by the same media that once mocked us.”

Truth, Even When It Hurts

I also knew that telling the truth comes at a cost.

The elites didn’t just control politics; they controlled perception. They weaponized division. They wanted us divided by race, class, religion, and party. They wanted us blind, distracted, fighting each other instead of them.

“End the Fed,” I said.

“Or charge them with treason.”

That was my stance. It still is.

The Biblical Undercurrent

Through every conversation, every post, and every digital skirmish, my foundation stayed the same: this was not just political—it was spiritual.

I often thought back to my childhood.

I remembered praying for wisdom during an AWANA meeting after hearing the story of Solomon for the first time. I didn’t fully understand it then, but I believe that prayer shaped me. It set me on a path where truth mattered more than comfort, where I would rather be hated for telling the truth than loved for believing lies.

And that’s exactly where God wanted me.

Chapter: Shadows and Signs

While the Q movement lit fires online, my real life was a storm of supernatural moments and raw lessons in leadership, love, and loss.

The Cabin and the Voice in the Night

After Wendy disappeared with the kids, I got an unexpected invitation to see them at the mountain cabin—as long as I didn’t bring beer or cigarettes. I laughed at that condition; it wasn’t a struggle.

Seeing my kids again was like breathing after drowning. The first night was beautiful, almost normal. By the second night, Wendy seduced me. But as soon as we were done, I noticed blood. She knew I didn’t believe in sex during her period according to Scripture. The thought hit me hard—was she pregnant with someone else’s child?

I said nothing. When she came home later, I never asked about it. I was just grateful my family was back.

That night, something else happened.

While sleeping in separate rooms, I heard my name.

“Eddie…”

The voice was eerie, like a ghost. It called me again and again, until I got out of bed and followed it. I traced it to Wendy’s room—she was sound asleep. The voice stopped. I stood there, paralyzed, knowing I had brushed up against something dark.

Later, Wendy said she felt released from demonic oppression that weekend. I believe I witnessed that battle in a way few ever do.

The Tree, the Crash, the Blood

Years later, working late on cannabis products for the local gas station, I stayed up too long and drove exhausted at dawn. Somewhere along the route, I fell asleep at the wheel and slammed head-on into a tree at full speed.

The Nissan Sentra crumpled.

Beer bottles—leftover from a friend detoxing at my farm—exploded. One shattered against my seat, embedding a jagged edge where my head had been. Another hit me in the back of the skull.

The cops who arrived laughed, thinking I was high. They didn’t know everything in my car was state-legal, tested by the Department of Agriculture. They arrested me instead of taking me to the hospital.

From jail, I called Wendy again and again. No answer. When she finally picked me up, I told her I needed the ER.

The doctor took one look and ordered a life flight.

“Mr. Martinie, if you don’t get on that helicopter, you’ll lose your leg.”

I argued about the cost.

“We’ll drive.”

He stared at me, dead serious.

“You won’t make it.”

I got on the helicopter.

A Cop, a Son, and a Breathalyzer That Never Came

Another time, I was driving with my son Zack, just 14 at the time. A cop pulled me over, shouting something about right-of-way. He ordered me out for a sobriety test. I passed two.

He still said he didn’t believe me.

“Give me a breathalyzer,” I demanded.

“Don’t have one,” he said. “I’m arresting you and taking you to the hospital.”

I pleaded:

“My son is right here—he can’t be left alone in the truck!”

He said he’d call another officer to stay with Zack. Watching my son’s face in the rearview mirror as I was cuffed and placed in the back of that car is an image burned into my soul. It was the face of fear, confusion, and heartbreak.

Moments like that change you.

Leadership and Intimacy in the Midst of Chaos

Throughout this period, my journals were filled with two recurring themes: leadership and intimacy in marriage.

I wrote about leadership because I was desperate to lead my family, my community, and even myself through storms that seemed never-ending. Leadership is not control—it’s service, sacrifice, and vision.

I wrote about intimacy because I longed for connection with my wife that wasn’t poisoned by anger, fear, or silence. My journals were full of prayers, strategies, and pleas for God to fix what I could not.

But Wendy never read them. She never answered my long emails or acknowledged the depth of what I was fighting for. Those words became my secret battle, my private war.

The Prayer That Started It All

Somewhere in those years, I remembered something from long before—the night in AWANA when I prayed for wisdom after hearing the story of Solomon. I was just a kid, but I believe that prayer set something in motion.

Wisdom is dangerous.

It opens your eyes to things you sometimes wish you couldn’t see.

But it also keeps you alive when the world wants you dead.
