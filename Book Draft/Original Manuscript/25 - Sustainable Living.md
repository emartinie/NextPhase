### Sustainable Living

### Wisdom Over Fear

The word prepping has become increasingly popular over the last several years. Depending on who you ask, it might mean storing food, preparing for disasters, living off-grid, or simply trying to become more self-sufficient. While there is certainly wisdom in being prepared, HomeGroups approaches the subject from a different perspective.

Our first priority has never been preparing for disaster. It has always been preparing people.

Throughout Scripture, God repeatedly calls His people to become wise, faithful stewards—people who know His Word, care for one another, and are ready to serve regardless of the circumstances around them. Spiritual preparedness always comes before physical preparedness.

A pantry full of food is valuable.

A heart full of God's Word is priceless.

The Bible never encourages believers to live in fear. Instead, it teaches us to live with wisdom.

Joseph prepared during years of abundance so others could survive years of famine.

Nehemiah rebuilt the walls of Jerusalem while encouraging the people to remain faithful.

The Proverbs repeatedly praise those who think ahead, work diligently, and steward their resources wisely.

The early believers cared for one another so that no one among them was left in need.

These examples are not about fear.

They are about faithful stewardship.

HomeGroups encourages believers to become resilient—not because we expect disaster around every corner, but because wisdom prepares us to love and serve others well in every season of life.

That kind of preparation begins long before purchasing supplies.

It begins by knowing God's Word.

Learning practical skills.

Strengthening our families.

Building relationships within our local communities.

Living generously.

Growing in wisdom.

When people know one another, study together, pray together, and serve together, they naturally become stronger during difficult seasons. Healthy communities have always been one of God's primary answers to uncertainty.

That is one of the reasons HomeGroups exists.

We believe discipleship should prepare believers for every part of life—not just Sunday mornings.

As we study Scripture together, we also learn how to steward our time, our resources, our relationships, and the gifts God has entrusted to us.

Our goal is not to create fearful people waiting for the world to collapse.

Our goal is to help cultivate mature disciples who remain steady when the world becomes uncertain.

There will always be headlines that encourage fear.

Wars.

Economic uncertainty.

Natural disasters.

Political division.

Every generation has faced its own challenges.

Rather than reacting to every headline, Scripture calls us to something better.

"Go to the ant, you sluggard; consider its ways and be wise..." (Proverbs 6:6)

"A prudent person foresees danger and takes precautions..." (Proverbs 22:3)

These verses are not invitations to fear.

They are invitations to wisdom.

So where do you begin?

Start where you are.

Become a student of God's Word.

Know my neighbors.

Strengthen my family.

Learn practical skills.

Practice generosity.

Become involved in a healthy biblical community.

Store what is wise to store.

Share what God has entrusted to you.

Encourage those around you.

One faithful step at a time.

Our hope is simple.

That believers would become deeply rooted in Scripture, grounded in truth, equipped to serve others, and prepared—both spiritually and practically—for whatever tomorrow brings.

Not because we fear the future.

Because we trust the One who already holds it.
