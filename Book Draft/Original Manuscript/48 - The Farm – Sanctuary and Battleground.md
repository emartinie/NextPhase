### The Farm – Sanctuary and Battleground

The farm was never just a piece of land. It was a mission.

Eddie spent three years chasing what many thought was an impossible dream—acquiring this unique property. The journey was paved with setbacks, small miracles, and connections that could only have been orchestrated by a higher hand. From unexpected ties to his former work at Gibson Guitars to perfectly timed coincidences, every step toward the farm seemed guided.

Once secured, the farm became a hub of innovation and community.

It wasn’t just crops in the soil—it was a world-class indoor grow room and showroom where Eddie cultivated cannabis under cutting-edge grow lights, blending science with agriculture. Deep water cultivation, hydroponics, aquaponics, and even aquaculture with a 50,000-gallon fish tank in the ground—this was a place where technology and nature thrived together. He even designed and sold home aquaponics units built by a friend and mentor in China, a man deeply involved in the underground church.

But the farm was also about people.

Every Saturday, the grounds filled with families, kids, and the elderly, all gathering for fishing tournaments where a hundred people might cast their lines in hopes of landing a trophy fish. For many, this was more than recreation; it was healing. Children laughed, the elderly found a safe place to reminisce, and special needs individuals were welcomed without reservation.

Community Meetings and Prepping

In addition to being a sanctuary, the farm became a training ground.

Eddie hosted community meetings to teach practical prepper skills: emergency communication, survival strategies, and self-reliance rooted in faith. He had terabytes of military and emergency documentation archived and ready to share. He trained people on amateur radios, creating networks of resilience long before such things were popular.

And yet, even within this haven, there were moments of deep conflict.

At one meeting, his wife stood up and told the attendees, “We were never preppers.”

The words stunned Eddie.

How could she deny what was right in front of them? The radios, the documents, the training—it was all real. To Eddie, this wasn’t just a difference of opinion; it was as if two realities were colliding.
