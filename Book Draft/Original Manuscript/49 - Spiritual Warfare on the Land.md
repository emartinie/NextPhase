### Spiritual Warfare on the Land

The farm’s purpose was clear to Eddie: it was a place of freedom and preparation. But it also became the stage for spiritual battles.

•	The Cabin Incident: On one of his visits to the mountain cabin where Wendy had taken the kids, Eddie awoke in the night to hear his name being called. The voice was eerie, almost ghostly, and led him to Wendy’s room—where she was sound asleep. The experience chilled him to the core. Later, Wendy confessed she felt freed from demonic oppression during that season. Eddie believes what he encountered that night was tied to her deliverance.

•	Oppression and Release: The farm attracted light, but light always draws opposition. There were days when the tension between spiritual forces felt as tangible as the soil beneath his feet.

A Safe Haven Amid the Storm

Despite the unseen battles, the farm fulfilled its mission. It offered a safe haven where people could laugh, learn, and grow together. It stood as proof that faith, innovation, and community could coexist.

For Eddie, it wasn’t just about prepping for emergencies—it was about preparing souls. The land itself became a testimony: that in the face of chaos, God always makes a way.
