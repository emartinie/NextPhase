### Chapter 16 – The Silence Years

There are moments that divide life into two parts.

Everything before.

Everything after.

For me, one of those moments came in the year 2000, standing in a courtroom as a judge determined where the next several years of my life would be spent.

I had explanations.

I had regrets.

I had hoped things would turn out differently.

None of that mattered.

### Looking Back

Today I see those early years differently.

The search itself changed me long before I found many of the answers I thought I was looking for.

Eventually I realized something that still shapes my life.

Truth isn't merely an idea to master.

Truth is Someone to know.

The search didn't end when I came to that realization.

It simply became deeper.

And it continues today.
