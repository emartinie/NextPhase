### Chapter  – HomeGroups.org: Church Beyond Walls

I’ve always believed that faith was meant to be lived, not confined. The idea of church as a building never fully captured what Jesus had in mind when He spoke of His followers as a body. Over the years, I saw more and more how traditional church structures were leaving people behind—those hurt by religion, those seeking something real, those who didn’t fit into the mold.

That’s when HomeGroups.org was born.

The Vision

2013–2018 Voice:

“Imagine a platform where believers could connect, grow, and serve—without walls, without denominational politics, without the distractions that keep so many from authentic community.”

HomeGroups.org started as a web app with one simple mission: to bring the church back to the people. It wasn’t about replacing local churches; it was about expanding the Kingdom. It provided tools for small groups, online gatherings, and community-driven discipleship.

It worked like a church app—but it was more. It didn’t stop at sermons and schedules; it empowered users to form meaningful groups, share resources, and live out their faith in their neighborhoods.

Building the Dream

With limited resources but unlimited vision, I built the app from the ground up.

•	It launched on Apple, Android, and international platforms.

•	It offered tools for leaders, resources for discipleship, and a hub for community.

•	Its web-based foundation meant it could be used anywhere, anytime.

For a while, it thrived. People connected. Groups formed. Faith grew in living rooms instead of pews.

The Prophetic Element

HomeGroups.org wasn’t just a tech project. It was prophetic.

The logo itself carried meaning—something God placed on my heart. It tied to Ezekiel 37, the vision of dry bones coming to life. The scattered, disconnected bones in Ezekiel’s vision were like the scattered believers I saw everywhere—isolated, unengaged, spiritually dry.

The app symbolized breath being brought back into the body of Christ. Not by human effort, but by the Spirit. What started as code became a prophetic declaration: God is rebuilding His church, piece by piece, home by home.

Challenges & Loss

The vision was strong, but reality hit hard. Financial hardship, personal struggles, and the complexity of keeping an app alive without steady funding took their toll. Hosting costs mounted. Marketing was nearly impossible on my own. Eventually, the app went offline.

But even as the servers shut down, the vision remained alive.

Looking Back, Looking Forward

2025 Reflection:

When I look at HomeGroups.org now, I don’t see a failure. I see a seed. It was ahead of its time, a glimpse of where the church is going. Today, with online communities flourishing, I realize that what I built was a precursor to movements we now take for granted.

The next version—if and when it comes—will be stronger. It will combine technology, discipleship, and prophecy in ways I could only dream of back then.

Because the church was never meant to be a building. It was meant to be a body. And bodies live and breathe—even when scattered.
