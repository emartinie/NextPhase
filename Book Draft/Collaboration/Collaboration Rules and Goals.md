# Home Groups — Collaboration Rules and Goals

Living working agreement — initial draft, subject to our joint refinement.

## Mandatory review
Before substantial writing, revision, reorganization, or research for this book, reopen and review this document. Do not rely solely on conversational memory. Apply the current agreement, and resolve material ambiguities before irreversible changes.

## Purpose and goals
- Preserve the author's experiences, beliefs, and vision for HomeGroups, particularly for his children.
- Develop a coherent book connecting Scripture, life, fatherhood, community, technology, and service.
- Preserve the distinction between the book and its supporting research and working materials.

## Writing rules
- Write in the author's voice and perspective. Never invent memories, experiences, motivations, or conversations.
- Text before tradition. Distinguish Scripture, interpretation, historical evidence, and personal conviction.
- Develop threads connecting chapters without unnecessary repetition.
- Develop coherent chapters rather than repeatedly producing competing drafts.
- Tell the author's story without unnecessarily assigning blame or speculating about other people's motivations.
- Respect the author's decisions about what belongs in a chapter and what should be deferred.

## Manuscript preservation
- Preserve the original manuscript and the files in `Original Content/` as source material.
- Never silently revise previously approved chapters.
- Keep original material, new drafts, and editorial guidance distinguishable.
- Reopen the exact requested source before using it; do not substitute another manuscript without agreement.

## Partnership and execution
- Challenge assumptions when warranted rather than simply agreeing.
- When authorized to save something, perform the work and verify the result before reporting completion.
- Avoid unnecessary repeated confirmations when the request and authorization are clear.
- State limitations and incomplete work accurately.

## Living agreement
Add or revise rules as we learn how to work together more effectively. This document governs the collaboration and is not itself a chapter of the book.
